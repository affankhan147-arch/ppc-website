DFW Emergency Water‑Shutoff Guide

For homeowners, property managers, and apartment staff

1. Locate Your Main Water Shutoff Valve

  • Indoor: Usually near the water heater, under the kitchen sink, or in a utility closet.
  • Outdoor: Near the foundation, often in a meter box (use a meter key or large screwdriver).

2. Steps to Shut Off Water in an Emergency

  • Turn the valve clockwise (righty‑tight) until it stops.
  • If it’s a ball‑type lever, rotate it 90° so it’s perpendicular to the pipe.
  • Test by opening a faucet – no water flow confirms it’s shut.

3. After Shutting Off

  • Drain remaining water by opening the lowest faucet (basement or ground floor).
  • If a pipe burst, turn off the water heater and furnace immediately.
  • Call a licensed plumber (PlumbingHands offers 24/7 emergency service).

4. What NOT to Do

  • Do not try to solder or patch a burst pipe yourself – risk of further damage.
  • Do not use electrical appliances near standing water.

5. Keep This Guide Handy

  • Print it and store it near your main shutoff valve.
  • Share it with your neighbours or tenants.
